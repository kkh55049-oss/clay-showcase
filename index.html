The Craft

A folk art passed down for generations in Fengxiang, Shaanxi — where clay is shaped into guardians of good fortune.

Fengxiang painted clay (凤翔泥塑) is one of China's best-loved folk arts, made for centuries in the villages around Baoji in Shaanxi province — the ancient heartland of Chinese civilisation. It is recognised as part of China's national intangible cultural heritage.

Each piece is pressed from local clay in hand-made molds, dried, coated in white, then outlined in black ink and painted in bold reds, greens, yellows and gold. The result is unmistakable: round, joyful forms and patterns of flowers and clouds that carry wishes for a good life.
